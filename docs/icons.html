ThemeLens icons

The icons of ThemeLens, drawn as SVG. The sources are in assets/icons, one file per icon. The app carries the app icon and the tray icons as .ico files in src/ThemeLens.App/Resources; build/make-icons.cjs and build/make-icons.py make them from the sources. The lens handle draws its two buttons itself, after the same shapes.

In the app today: the app icon (window, taskbar, executable), the three tray icons, and the handle's four button pictures (edit mode, done, fill this screen, back to its size). The other command icons are drawn and waiting for the places that will use them.

App icon

A light page with a lens over it. Outside the lens the text is dark on light; inside, the same lines are light on black. The amber bar is the handle.

Three states, told apart by shape, not by colour alone: a filled lens with lines when it is on, an empty outline when it is off, and a filled lens with a pause mark while it holds the background.
