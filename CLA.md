# Contributor agreement (draft)

> **Draft, not yet in force.** This text has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It must be reviewed before the
> project accepts outside contributions or sells licences (see section 12 of the specification). Until then,
> please open an issue before starting work on a contribution.

The intent, in plain words:

1. You keep the copyright in your contribution.
2. You license your contribution to the project under GPL-3.0-or-later, like the rest of the code.
3. You also give the project owner the right to license your contribution under other terms, including
   commercial ones. This is what keeps commercial licensing possible without asking every contributor to agree
   to each deal.
4. You confirm that the contribution is your own work, or that you have the right to submit it.

The binding text will replace this section once it has been reviewed.
